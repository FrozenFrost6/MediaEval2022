## Introduction 
 The need to organize prevalent multimedia in our day-to-day activities, presents us various aspects of
 video importance to be taken into consideration, like video aesthetics and interestingness. Memorability
 based organization of media is highly effective in cases where media is required to have a long positive
 retainability in one’s memory, like in the field of advertising or educational content creation. Keeping our
 target group in mind, we have decided to pursue an ensemble-based approach for our model for the task
 of predicting media memorability in aide to the Benchmarking Initiative for Multimedia Evaluation’s
 list of tasks for the MediaEval 2022 Workshop
